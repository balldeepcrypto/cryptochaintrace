Cluster Finder excludes only wallets classified as known exchanges. All non-exchange labels, including individual handles, remain eligible on every chain, whether used as a lead, input, focus wallet, or discovered proxy. This replaces the earlier label whitelist and specific Lattice Treasury exception.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested exchange-only exclusion across chains and inclusion of named individuals such as Gen.BearJew, Dagnum, JustLiveIt, Prophet, and BitcoinBarry.

**How to apply:** Use exchange classification rather than label-text matching. Do not infer that a named account is an exchange, official organization, or suspicious wallet merely from its name. Keep catalog labels unchanged when editing this filter.

The separate printable exchange section includes observed IN/OUT transfers for all known exchanges, not just US exchanges, with full transaction details.

**Why:** The user broadened the requested exchange report scope while keeping the report structure unchanged.

**How to apply:** Keep exchanges out of expansion, retain their observed touches separately, and preserve exact exchange labels. Older snapshots may contain only the exchange observations collected under their original rules.
